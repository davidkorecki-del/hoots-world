## CONCEPTS

### Reel 1 — "THE FOG LIFTS"
- **Pitch:** A boy's voice reads "Be still" over a road drowned in fog — and as the verse unfolds, the fog literally dissolves into a clear, sunlit road. The scripture does on screen exactly what it says.
- **Hook (first 2 s, on screen + spoken):** "For the loudest day of your week."
- **Why it works:** The transformation IS the message. Viewers stay to watch the fog clear, and the verse lands at the exact moment it does.

### Reel 2 — "COUNTED IN SHEEP"
- **Pitch:** A child's voice reads "I shall lack nothing" over a golden pasture filling with grazing sheep — abundance you can literally count, building as the promise is spoken.
- **Hook (first 2 s, on screen + spoken):** "Listen to this promise."
- **Why it works:** The promise is shown, not just said. The flock makes "lack nothing" tangible in a way no verse card can.

### Reel 3 — "THE CANDLE THAT STAYS LIT"
- **Pitch:** One real flame refuses to go out in the dark while an adult voice reads Isaiah's "great light" — then the same unbroken flame keeps burning as a child's voice begins the Christmas story itself.
- **Hook (first 2 s):** The verse opens at 0.3 s over the flame: "The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light."
- **Why it works:** Advent anticipation in a single uncut shot — the flame carries 35 seconds without a cut because it's really alive, and the story excerpt proves the product, not just the mood.

### Photo 1 — "FIRST LIGHT, FIRST MILE"
- **Pitch:** Dawn through a truck windshield, coffee steaming on the dash — the day starts with a promise before the engine does.
- **Why it stops the scroll:** Everyone knows this exact seat. The verse is set in the dark of the dash, where the driver's eyes actually rest.

### Photo 2 — "THE FRIDGE-DOOR SERMON"
- **Pitch:** The verse where a family actually lives — handwritten on a sticky note on the fridge, right beside a child's crayon drawing of home.
- **Why it stops the scroll:** It looks found, not made. Handwriting on a real note sells the everyday-devotion idea better than any layout.

### Photo 3 — "OPEN ALL NIGHT"
- **Pitch:** A rain-soaked roadside diner glowing on a dark road, its neon sign burning EAT into the night — the only light for miles is an invitation.
- **Why it stops the scroll:** Cinematic night photography + real neon lettering + "A light for my path" — the scene and the verse are the same picture.

### Photo ideas beyond the rendered three (concepts, not templates)
- **"The Receipt"** — Psalm 46:10 written on the back of a gas-station receipt at midnight, coffee ring on the counter. *Stops the scroll because scripture turns up in the exact place anxiety lives: on the road, at night, mid-journey.*
- **"Driveway Chalk"** — "I shall lack nothing" in a child's chalk handwriting across a driveway at golden hour, hopscotch grid beside it. *Stops the scroll because the verse sits at child height, in a child's hand — disarming and instantly shareable by parents.*
- **"Rear-View Mirror"** — Sunrise behind the car, a verse card tucked into the rear-view mirror, the road already travelled soft in the glass. *Stops the scroll because the mirror forces a double-take: looking back at how far you've come.*

## Agency choices & production notes (v2)

- **Campaign look in one line:** warm golden-hour grade on daylight, warm amber on night, one typography system (Cormorant Garamond off-white #FFF6E0 + gold #E8B95A letter-tracked references + Montserrat), one brand card — six pieces, one campaign.
- **Grade applied to all footage and photos:** gentle contrast (1.05–1.06), lifted blacks, +10–12% saturation, warm color balance (ffmpeg eq/curves/colorbalance).
- **Fonts (in `fonts/`):** Cormorant Garamond (display serif + italic), Montserrat (sans, references, brand card), Caveat (handwriting, fridge note). No default fonts anywhere; text rendered as designed overlays so spelling is exact; no opaque black boxes — soft gradient scrims and shadows only.
- **Music bed:** a gentle solo-piano performance generated for this campaign, extracted and seam-crossfaded into a loop, low-passed at 1100 Hz, sidechain-ducked under the narration (≈ −20 dB under voice), faded in/out per Reel.
- **Sound gate (measured on the final files):** Reel 1: −16.5 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.5 dBTP, first voice onset ≈ 0.3 s. Reel 2: −17.9 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP, onset ≈ 0.3 s. Reel 3: −16.7 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP, onset ≈ 0.35 s. All three: AAC audio stream present, H.264 1080×1920, yuv420p, faststart verified (moov before mdat).
- **Final specs (ffprobe):** Reel 1: 12.97 s / 30.2 MB · Reel 2: 13.0 s / 23.2 MB · Reel 3: 35.0 s / 23.9 MB. Photos: 1080×1350 PNG (1.0 / 1.5 / 1.8 MB).
- **Story excerpt source:** the real delivered recording `~/workspace/long-road-batch1/out/sample1/stories/32.m4a`; the v2 excerpt runs 0.3 s → 23.3 s of that file with short fades.
- **QA:** frames extracted at ~1 s, mid and end-card from every Reel (see `qa/`) and all three photos were visually inspected: no black boxes, no default font, no empty sign, no garbled text, captions clear of subjects, brand cards clean.
