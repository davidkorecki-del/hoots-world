## W1 — THE MIRROR NOTE (01-mirror-note.png)
**In the image:** a yellow sticky note on a bathroom mirror in morning light, makeup bag beside it, handwritten: *"I am fearfully and wonderfully made."*

**Hook:** The first face you see every morning is yours. God had something to say about her before the day says anything.

**Verse (read it out loud):**
"I will give thanks to you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Your works are wonderful. My soul knows that very well."
— Psalm 139:14 (WEB)

**In plain words:** You were not mass-produced. You were made — carefully, on purpose — and the One who made you calls the work wonderful.

**Save/send:** Save this for the morning you don't believe it. Send it to a daughter, a sister, a friend who needs to hear she's made on purpose.
